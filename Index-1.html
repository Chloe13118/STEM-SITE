Our research question for this experiment was ‘ What substance melts ice the fastest?’ ,

and our hypothesis was that salt will melt the ice faster.

We started this experiment by having curiosity about why people use salt when melting ice in winter seasons.

For the independent variables, we chose salt (our hypothesis), sugar, soda, and pure water.

500ml measuring cylinder, 50ml beaker cups, lab coat, goggles, and gloves were added to our experiment for methods.

For the accuracy of this experiment, we divided the whole experiment into 3: A,B,C.

Our overall method was pouring the same amount of water and substances when dealing with

substances and having the same amount of liquid when measuring on liquid(control

variables: same amount of ice). After that, we dropped similar sizes of ice cubes at

the same time and started the timer(dependent variables:time). To start with, we made

three different temperatures (A): Hot water, Cold water and Room temperature water.

From this experiment we acknowledged that hot water melts the ice fastest, followed by room

temperature water and then cold water. In experiment B, it was measured by the states of matter,

water, air, and salt(solid). For the result, room temperature water melted ice the fastest followed by

salt and just ice. Finally, on experiment C, it was measured by 4 different substances, leading from room temperature

to salt to soda to sugar. In total, hot water was the best way to melt ice by only taking 1 minutes and 55 seconds.

That disproves our hypothesis because that hot water was the most reasonable way to melt ice quickly.
